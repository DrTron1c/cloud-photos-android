# Cloud Photos change log

## Build 24 · Memories, People & Pets, Places and big-screen mode
- **Memories** — trips, big days, seasons, years and On This Day, made on this device, played with music (three built-in tracks or your own song). Optional morning **On This Day** reminder.
- **People & Pets** — faces found and grouped on the device; name people, merge, hide. Search “Anna beach 2025”. Dogs and cats found by Smart Search.
- **Text in photos** — read signs, documents and screenshots: search their words, or long-press a photo to select its text (Live Text).
- **Places** — photos grouped by town (named offline), with an optional map — place names in English.
- **Similar Photos** — bursts and retakes grouped so you can keep the best and clear the rest.
- **Full screen** — a tap hides everything, including the video bar; zooming in hides it too. Rounded icon in Recents.
- **Zoom** — zoom much further into photos and videos; photos load full-resolution detail as you zoom, and videos zoom at their own resolution.
- **Shared Albums** — add photos (from your library or this device), like and comment, using the same requests as icloud.com. New — tell me if anything misbehaves.
- **Videos** — always shown whole, fitted to the screen whatever their shape (portrait phone videos on a tablet, landscape on a phone); pinch to zoom, pinch out to see it all again. **GIFs play like videos**, with the same scrub bar, frame strip, frame-by-frame buttons and speeds. A **temporary cache** (Settings › Temporary cache) keeps videos and GIFs you've watched for a while, so replaying and scrubbing don't download them again. The picture follows your finger as you drag the progress bar; a strip of the video's real frames to scrub through and frame-by-frame buttons when paused; picture-in-picture, playback speed, save a frame, trim to a new clip.
- **Where was this?** — the globe button on a photo or video shows where it was taken on the map, with “Photos Taken Here”.
- **Tablets** — iPad-style sidebar, photos open beside the grid, highest refresh rate (e.g. 144 Hz), keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+/), drag photos into other apps and drop photos in to upload.
- **More** — export to any folder, share HEIC as JPEG, Quick Settings tile for Proton VPN, long-press app-icon shortcuts.
- **Covers** — Memories, People & Pets, Places and Similar Photos always show their own photos, even right after photos are added or removed on another device (Similar Photos' clean-up only ever deletes the exact photos shown).
- **Privacy** — all of this runs on the device; Hidden and Recently Deleted photos are never analysed, and anything learned about a photo is wiped when you hide or delete it.

## Build 21 · Smart Search, Liquid Glass, Proton VPN
- **Smart Search (on-device AI)** — search what's in your photos, like “red hat late 2026” or “dog on a beach summer 2025”. A small AI model looks at your photos on this device; nothing about your photos or searches leaves it. Turn it on in Search or Settings › Smart Search.
- **Date words in search** — “early / mid / late 2026”, seasons, “last month”, “this year”, “christmas”, “weekend” and decades like “2010s”.
- **Liquid Glass** — see-through glass tab bar, buttons, toolbars, viewer bars and banners, like iOS 26. Switch it in Settings › Appearance.
- **Update banner** — a banner at the top tells you when a new build is out; download and install it right there, and read what's new first.
- **Proton VPN** — connect your Proton VPN account (Settings › Privacy & security › Proton VPN), pick a country or the closest free one, and have it connect when Cloud Photos opens and disconnect when you leave. By default only Cloud Photos goes through it.

## Build 17 · Your own app icon and name
- **App icon & name** — type any name and use any picture (or one of your photos) as the app's icon; it's added as a small companion app on your home screen and app drawer.

## Build 9 · Big update
- **Years, Months, Days** — browse your library like iPhone, with swipe-to-select, albums, shared albums, offline albums, auto-upload, slideshows, edits, a home-screen widget, search and lots of settings.
