# Cloud Photos change log

## Build 20 · Smart Search, Liquid Glass, Proton VPN
- **Smart Search (on-device AI)** — search what's in your photos, like “red hat late 2026” or “dog on a beach summer 2025”. A small AI model looks at your photos on this device; nothing about your photos or searches leaves it. Turn it on in Search or Settings › Smart Search.
- **Date words in search** — “early / mid / late 2026”, seasons, “last month”, “this year”, “christmas”, “weekend” and decades like “2010s”.
- **Liquid Glass** — see-through glass tab bar, buttons, toolbars, viewer bars and banners, like iOS 26. Switch it in Settings › Appearance.
- **Update banner** — a banner at the top tells you when a new build is out; download and install it right there, and read what's new first.
- **Proton VPN** — connect your Proton VPN account (Settings › Privacy & security › Proton VPN), pick a country or the closest free one, and have it connect when Cloud Photos opens and disconnect when you leave. By default only Cloud Photos goes through it.

## Build 17 · Your own app icon and name
- **App icon & name** — type any name and use any picture (or one of your photos) as the app's icon; it's added as a small companion app on your home screen and app drawer.

## Build 9 · Big update
- **Years, Months, Days** — browse your library like iPhone, with swipe-to-select, albums, shared albums, offline albums, auto-upload, slideshows, edits, a home-screen widget, search and lots of settings.
